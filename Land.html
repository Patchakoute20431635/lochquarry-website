Land Based Activities

Hillwalking

From short walks around the site to Munro‑bagging expeditions, tailored to suit all ages and experience levels. Max group size: 12. Ages 6+.

Archery

Learn to safely use a bow and arrow and take part in fun shoot‑out competitions. Max group size: 6. Ages 6+.

Orienteering

Navigate the centre grounds, find the markers and make it back in time. Groups of 2 or more. Ages 6+.

Axe Throwing

Try your hand at Viking‑style axe throwing and aim for the bullseye. Max group size: 6. Ages 10+.
